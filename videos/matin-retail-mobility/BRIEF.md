## Intent

A video version of the deck "Matin, Lululemon: Modernizing Retail Mobility." It tells Matin's
story of moving Lululemon from an aging MDM platform to ienterprise (Enterprise Manager for the
people who run the fleet, Site Manager for the people who run the store). It plays on a 16:9
event screen or website at roughly 2.5 minutes, covering every slide including the seven
takeaways for other retailers. Music bed plus kinetic on-screen type, no voiceover.
Apple keynote design language: clean, confident, executive.
